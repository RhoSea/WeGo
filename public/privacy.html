WeGo — Privacy

Last updated 29 August 2026

WeGo is a small tool that lets a group of friends plan one trip together and keep track of what each person still needs to save. This page explains exactly what it stores and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, provided by Google when you sign in. This is the only thing we request from your Google account, and it is used solely to identify you to the other people on your trip.
  • The trip information you type in: plan ideas, estimated and actual costs, and the savings amounts you choose to record.

What we never collect

WeGo does not ask for and cannot store bank details, card numbers, passwords, or identity documents. It does not move, hold, or process money. The savings figures are notes you type for your own tracking — nothing more.

Who can see your data

Only people who have joined the same trip as you. This is enforced by the database itself through row-level security policies, not merely hidden in the interface. People who are not members of your trip cannot read it, and each invitation link works only once, for one person.

Your savings entries are visible to your trip members, since the point is to see how the group is doing, but only you can add, change, or delete your own.

Where it is stored

Trip data is held in a Supabase project (hosted PostgreSQL) belonging to the person who set up this instance of WeGo. Sign-in is handled by Google. No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything beyond running the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete any plan item, cost, or savings entry from within the app at any time. To remove your account and everything associated with it, contact the person who runs this instance, listed below.
